# Coffee & Artisan Roastery Guide

## Extraction Parameters
- **Kyoto Slow Drip**: 12 hours Dutch cold steep, 1 drop per 1.5s, Yirgacheffe Ethiopia.
- **Panama Geisha**: Hand-poured V60, 93°C water, 2,100m Boquete terroir.
- **Ceremonial Matcha**: Stone-ground first flush tencha from Uji, Kyoto.
- **Sourdough & Pastry**: 72-hour cold fermented dough with cultured Normandy lactic butter.
